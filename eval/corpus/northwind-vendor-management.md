# Northwind Surveying Co. — Vendor Management Policy

*Owner: Procurement. Last revised: 2025.*

Any new vendor contract over $10,000 annually requires approval from both
the requesting department head and Finance. Vendors handling customer
survey data must sign a data processing agreement and pass an IT Security
review before onboarding, following the same MFA and encryption standards
required internally under the Information Security Policy.

Existing vendor contracts are reviewed annually each March. A vendor found
non-compliant with the data processing agreement has 30 days to remediate
or the contract is terminated.
