# Northwind Surveying Co. — Expense Reimbursement Policy

*Owner: Finance. Last revised: 2025.*

Employees may submit expense reports for approved business costs: travel,
client meals (up to $75 per person), field equipment under $200, and
professional development up to $1,000 per year. Reports must be submitted
within 60 days of the expense date through the Finance portal; requests
submitted later are not processed.

Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from a manager before the cost is
incurred. Mileage for personal vehicle use on client site visits is
reimbursed at the standard IRS rate, updated annually every January.
