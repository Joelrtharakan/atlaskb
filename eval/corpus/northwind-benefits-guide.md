# Northwind Surveying Co. — Health Benefits Guide

*Owner: People Operations. Last revised: 2025.*

Full-time employees are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage
starting on their first day of employment — there is no waiting period.
The company covers 80% of the premium for employees and 50% for dependents.
Open enrollment runs each November 1-15; changes outside that window
require a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, loss of other coverage).

Employees working fewer than 30 hours per week are not eligible for health
benefits, but remain eligible for the 401(k) plan described separately.
