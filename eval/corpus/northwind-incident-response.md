# Northwind Surveying Co. — Security Incident Response Runbook

*Owner: IT Security. Last revised: 2025.*

When a security incident is reported (per the Information Security Policy),
IT Security follows four stages: **Contain** (isolate the affected device or
account within 1 hour of confirmation), **Assess** (determine what data or
systems were exposed, within 4 hours), **Notify** (inform affected employees
and, if customer data is involved, the Legal team, within 24 hours), and
**Remediate** (patch the root cause and document lessons learned within 5
business days).

A stolen or lost laptop is treated as a confirmed incident automatically,
not just a suspected one, because full-disk encryption cannot be verified
remotely. The device's access credentials are revoked immediately upon
report, before containment is even confirmed.
