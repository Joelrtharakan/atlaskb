# Northwind Surveying Co. — Information Security Policy

*Owner: IT Security. Last revised: 2025.*

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and a screen lock
that activates after 5 minutes of inactivity. Passwords must be at least 12
characters and are rotated every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is
required for all systems that hold customer data, including the survey data
warehouse and the billing platform.

Employees may not install unapproved software on company devices without
IT approval. USB storage devices are disabled by default on all laptops;
exceptions require a signed request to IT Security.

Any suspected security incident (lost device, phishing email, unauthorized
access) must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours. See the Incident
Response Runbook for the escalation process.
