# Northwind Surveying Co. — PTO Policy (HR Handbook)

*Owner: People Operations. Last revised: March 2025.*

All full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of 1.5 days per
month, up to an annual cap of 18 days. Unused PTO above the cap does not roll
over into the next calendar year. Requests must be submitted through the HR
portal at least two weeks in advance for approval by a manager.
